> **A community where builders and makers come together to code, create, and collaborate.**

## 💎 What is Ametrine.club?

Ametrine.club is a global network of coding clubs and maker spaces where members learn to code through building projects, participating in hackathons, and growing together as a community.

## 🚀 Our Mission

To empower the next generation of builders by providing resources, community, and opportunities to create technology projects in a supportive, collaborative environment.

## 🌟 What We Plan To Offer

- **Local Clubs:** Community-led chapters meeting regularly in schools, libraries, community centers, and online
- **Workshops:** Beginner-friendly coding tutorials and project-based learning resources
- **Hackathons:** In-person and virtual events where members can build incredible projects in a limited timeframe
- **Community:** A vibrant online space where members can share work, get feedback, and collaborate
- **Mentorship:** Connection with experienced developers and industry professionals
